# Reverie

A quiet companion for musical sketching that runs entirely in your browser: it listens while you play the piano, transcribes what you play, and keeps a journal and a library of your ideas. Nothing is uploaded; your sketches live in your browser's storage.

Open the GitHub Pages site in desktop Chrome or Edge and allow the microphone. A sample journal to browse first: https://mtworth.github.io/reverie-sample-journal/
